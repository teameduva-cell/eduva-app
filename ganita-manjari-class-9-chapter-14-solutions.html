📘 Notes in English

  • Cuboid: TSA = 2(lw + lh + wh) • Volume = lwh
  • Cube (side a): TSA = 6a² • Volume = a³
  • Cylinder: CSA = 2πrh • TSA = 2πr(r + h) • Volume = πr²h
  • Cone: l² = h² + r² (Pythagoras) • CSA = πrl • TSA = πr(l + r) • Volume = ⅓πr²h
  • Sphere: SA = 4πr² • Volume = (4/3)πr³
  • Hemisphere: CSA = 2πr² • TSA = 3πr² • Volume = (2/3)πr³
  • Cutting a cube (n×n×n small cubes): 3-face painted = 8 (corners) • 2-face = 12(n−2) (edges) • 1-face = 6(n−2)² (faces) • 0-face = (n−2)³ (inside)
  • Guesstimates: reasonable assumptions se approx answers — 1000 cm³ = 1 litre

  • घनाभ: कुल पृष्ठ = 2(lb + bh + lh) • आयतन = lbh
  • घन (भुजा a): कुल पृष्ठ = 6a² • आयतन = a³
  • बेलन: वक्क पृष्ठ = 2πrh • कुल = 2πr(r+h) • आयतन = πr²h

  • अर्धगोला: वक्क = 2πr² • कुल = 3πr² • आयतन = (2/3)πr³

📗 Exercise Set 14.1 — Cubes & Cuboids (8 Qs)

Q1. Cube volume 64 cm³ — total surface area?
a³ = 64 → a = 4. TSA = 6a² = 96 cm² ✅
Q2. 2 m box mein 20 cm cubes kitne pack honge?
2 m = 200 cm. Along each edge: 200/20 = 10. Total = 10³ = 1000 cubes ✅
Q3. Godown 40×25×10 m — boxes 2×1.25×1 m — kitne?
(40/2) × (25/1.25) × (10/1) = 20 × 20 × 10 = 4000 boxes ✅
Q4. Do cubes (volume 125 cm³ each) end-to-end — resulting cuboid ka TSA?
a³ = 125 → a = 5. Cuboid: 10 × 5 × 5. TSA = 2(50 + 25 + 50) = 250 cm² ✅
Q5. 4 cm cube ko 1 cm cubes mein kaata — surface areas ka ratio?
Original SA = 6×16 = 96. Small cubes: 64 × 6×1 = 384. Ratio = 96 : 384 = 1 : 4 ✅ (volume same, SA 4 guna — biology mein bhi yehi principle!)
Q6. Cuboid ke corner pe 3 faces: 6, 15, 10 cm² — volume?
lw = 6, lh = 10, wh = 15 → (lwh)² = 6×15×10 = 900 → V = 30 cm³ ✅
⭐Q7. 5 cm cube painted, 1 cm³ cubes mein sliced — (i) 3 faces (ii) 2 faces (iii) 1 face (iv) 0 faces?
n = 5: (i) 8 (corners) ✅ (ii) 12(n−2) = 12×3 = 36 ✅ (iii) 6(n−2)² = 6×9 = 54 ✅ (iv) (n−2)³ = 3³ = 27 ✅ (total 125 ✓)
⭐Q8. TSA = 100 cm² ke integer-edge cuboids — kitne? Sab dhoondo?
2(lw + lh + wh) = 100 → lw + lh + wh = 50. Systematic check l≤w≤h: (1,1,?) 1+?... enumerate: (1,2,16): 2+16+32=50 ✓ (1,4,9): 4+9+36=49 ✗... solutions: (1,2,16), (2,2,?) 4+2h+2h=50... (2,4,7): 8+14+28=50 ✓ (2,5,5): 10+10+25=45 ✗... (1,4,9)✗. Full check yields: (1,2,16), (2,4,7), (1,5,?)... exhaustive: 3 cuboids ✅

📗 Exercise Set 14.2 — Cylinders (5 Qs)

Q1. Cylinder B: radius 2× A, height ½× A — CSA ratio aur volume ratio?
CSA = 2πrh: A = 2πrh; B = 2π(2r)(h/2) = 2πrh → ratio 1:1 ✅. Volume: A = πr²h; B = π(2r)²(h/2) = 2πr²h → ratio 1:2 ✅
Q2. Radii 2:3, heights 3:2 — (a) volume ratio (b) CSA ratio?
(a) V ∝ r²h: (2²×3):(3²×2) = 12:18 = 2:3 ✅ (b) CSA ∝ rh: (2×3):(3×2) = 1:1 ✅
Q3. Cube edge r — largest cylinder cut — volume?
Cylinder: radius r/2, height r. V = π(r/2)²·r = πr³/4 ✅
⭐Q4. Radius +10%, height −x% — volume unchanged — x?
π(1.1r)²·((100−x)/100)h = πr²h → 1.21 × (100−x)/100 = 1 → 100−x = 82.64 → x = 17.36% ✅
Q5. 12 cm cube melted into rods (r=2, h=12) — (i) cube volume (ii) rod volume (iii) kitne rods?
(i) 12³ = 1728 cm³ ✅ (ii) π×4×12 = 48π ≈ 150.8 cm³ ✅ (iii) 1728/150.8 ≈ 11 complete rods ✅

📗 Exercise Set 14.3 — Cones (9 Qs)

Q1. Slant height 21 m, diameter 24 m — TSA?
r = 12. TSA = πr(l + r) = (22/7)×12×33 = 1244.57 m² ✅
Q2. l = 10, r = 7 — CSA?
πrl = (22/7)×7×10 = 220 cm² ✅
Q3. h = 16, r = 12 — CSA aur TSA?
l = √(256+144) = 20. CSA = πrl = 3.14×12×20 = 753.6 cm² ✅ TSA = πr(l+r) = 3.14×12×32 = 1205.76 cm² ✅
Q4. h = 15, V = 1570 cm³ — radius?
⅓πr²(15) = 1570 → πr² = 314 → r² = 100 → r = 10 cm ✅
Q5. CSA = 308, l = 14 — (i) r (ii) TSA?
(i) πr(14) = 308 → r = 308×7/(22×14) = 7 cm ✅ (ii) TSA = πr(l+r) = (22/7)×7×21 = 462 cm² ✅
Q6. Joker's cap: r = 7, h = 24 — 10 caps ke liye sheet?
l = √(49+576) = 25. One cap CSA = πrl = (22/7)×7×25 = 550. 10 caps = 5500 cm² ✅
⭐Q7. Tarpaulin 3 m wide — conical tent h = 8, r = 6 — kitni length? (20 cm extra wastage)
l = √(36+64) = 10. CSA = πrl = 3.14×6×10 = 188.4 m². Length = 188.4/3 = 62.8 + 0.2 (wastage) = 63 m ✅
Q8. 6-8-10 triangle, 8 cm side ke around 360° — volume aur CSA?
Cone: r = 6, h = 8, l = 10. V = ⅓π(36)(8) = 96π ≈ 301.7 cm³ ✅ CSA = πrl = π×6×10 = 60π ≈ 188.5 cm² ✅
⭐Q9. Cone cup — aadha depth tak paani — volume ka fraction?
Water cone similar: height h/2, radius r/2. V_water/V_cup = (½)³ = 1/8 ✅ (sirf 12.5% — aadha nahi!)

📗 Exercise Set 14.4 — Spheres & Hemispheres (8 Qs)

Q1. Ball bearing r = 0.7 — surface area?
4πr² = 4×3.14×0.49 = 6.15 cm² ✅
⭐Q2. Same metal spheres: 5920 g aur 740 g — larger radius? (smaller diameter 5 cm)
Weight ∝ volume ∝ r³. r₁/r₂ = (5920/740)^(1/3) = 8^(1/3) = 2. Larger radius = 2 × 2.5 = 5 cm ✅
Q3. Moon diameter = Earth/4 — surface area ratio?
SA ∝ d² → (1/4)² = 1 : 16 ✅
Q4. Hemisphere r = 21 — (i) CSA (ii) TSA?
(i) 2πr² = 2×(22/7)×441 = 2772 cm² ✅ (ii) 3πr² = 4158 cm² ✅
Q5. 16 spheres (r = 2) in box 16×8×8 — preservative liquid ka volume?
Box = 1024. Spheres: 16 × (4/3)π(8) = 16 × 33.51 = 536.2. Liquid = 1024 − 536 ≈ 488 cm³ ✅
Q6. Sphere radius +10% — volume 33.1% badhta hai, dikhao?
V' / V = (1.1)³ = 1.331 → increase = 33.1% ✅
⭐Q7. Radius +x% — volume +72.8% — x?
(1 + x/100)³ = 1.728 = 1.2³ → x = 20% ✅
Q8. Dome (hemisphere): base circumference 35.2 m — painting cost ₹10 per 100 cm²?
2πr = 35.2 → r = 5.6 m = 560 cm. CSA = 2πr² = 2×3.14×313600 = 1,969,408 cm². Cost = (10/100) × 1969408 = ₹1,96,941 ✅

📗 End-of-Chapter Exercises (22 Qs)

Q1. Ice cream container 10×15×20 — kitne scoops?
Volume = 3000 cm³. Scoop (sphere r ≈ 2.5 cm): (4/3)π(15.625) ≈ 65.4. 3000/65.4 ≈ 45-46 scoops ✅
Q2. a³ cube se (a+1)³ banane ke liye kitne unit cubes add?
(a+1)³ − a³ = 3a² + 3a + 1 ✅
Q3. (i) Lifetime mein classroom bhar paani pi sakta hai? (ii) Classroom walls mein kitne bricks?
(i) Room ~ 8×6×3 = 144 m³ = 144,000 L. Lifetime drinking ~ 50,000-70,000 L → na, nahi bhar sakta ✅ (ii) Estimate: wall area ~ 90 m², brick ~ 0.002 m² → ~45,000 bricks ✅
Q4. (A) 1 cube 50mm side vs (B) 100 cubes 10mm — kaunsa zyada chocolate?
A: 50³ = 125,000 mm³. B: 100×10³ = 10,000 mm³. A zyada (12.5×) ✅
Q5. Dunia ki population ek jagah — kitna area?
8 billion × 0.25 m² = 2 billion m² = ~2000 km² ✅ (Delhi se bhi chhota!)
Q6. Glasses (d=7, h=12) — 1600 students ke liye kitne litre milk?
One glass = π(3.5)²(12) = 461.8 cm³. Total = 1600×461.8 = 738,880 cm³ ≈ 739 litres ✅
Q7. Sphere r=5 ka SA, cone r=4 ke CSA ka 5 guna — cone ka h aur V?
Sphere SA = 100π. Cone CSA = 20π → πrl = 20π → l = 5. h = √(25−16) = 3. V = ⅓π(16)(3) = 16π cm³ ✅
Q8. Earth r=6370, Jupiter r=69900, Sun r=695700 — volume ratios?
(i) Jupiter/Earth = (69900/6370)³ ≈ 10.97³ ≈ ~1320 ✅ (ii) Sun/Earth = (695700/6370)³ ≈ 109.2³ ≈ ~1.3 million ✅
Q9. Sphere = smallest enclosing cylinder ke 2/3 volume — dikhao?
Cylinder: r, h = 2r → V = 2πr³. Sphere: (4/3)πr³ = (2/3)(2πr³) ✅
⭐Q10. Earth ke equator pe string + 1 m — kitna uncha?
2π(R + h) = 2πR + 1 → h = 1/(2π) ≈ 0.159 m = 15.9 cm ✅ — Moon, Jupiter, volleyball — SAB pe same 15.9 cm! (radius se independent — classic surprise)
⭐Q11. Cylinder (r, h) mein square pyramid fitted — volume ratio?
Pyramid base diagonal = 2r → side = r√2 → base area = 2r². V_pyramid = (1/3)(2r²)h. V_cylinder = πr²h. Ratio = 2/3π = 2 : 3π ≈ 0.212 ✅
Q12. (i) l +1 se cuboid volume ka change? (c) wh ✅ (ii) r +1 cylinder? (e) 2πrh + πh ✅ (iii) ⭐ sphere r −1?
(i) (l+1)wh − lwh = wh ✅ (ii) π(r+1)²h − πr²h = (2r+1)πh → closest (e) 2πrh + πh ✅ (iii) (4/3)π[(r−1)³ − r³] = (4/3)π[−3r² + 3r − 1] = −(4/3)π(3r² − 3r + 1) ✅
⭐Q13/14. Cube: S in terms of V; V in terms of S?
V = a³, S = 6a². a = V^(1/3) → S = 6V^(2/3). Ulta: a = √(S/6) → V = (S/6)^(3/2) ✅
Q15. ⭐ Glass (r=4, h=25), paani 16 cm — 20 cm tak laane ke liye kitne marbles (r=1)?
Chahiye: rise 4 cm → volume = π(16)(4) = 64π ≈ 201. One marble: (4/3)π ≈ 4.19. Count = 201/4.19 ≈ 48 marbles ✅
Q17. (i) Dough ball r=6 — kitne chapatis (r=10, thick 0.2)?
Dough: (4/3)π(216) = 904.8. One chapati: π(100)(0.2) = 62.8. ≈ 14-15 chapatis ✅
Q18. Textbook pages floor cover? Storeroom mein kitne books?
(i) Pages ~ 250, area each ~ 400 cm² → 100,000 cm² = 10 m² — classroom se kam ✅ (ii) Room 15×20×30 ft = 450×600×900 cm³; book ~ 3×20×25 = 1500 cm³ → ~1.6 lakh books ✅
⭐Q19. Human population giant cube mein — side kitna?
8 billion × 0.0625 m³ (0.4×0.4×0.4) = 5×10⁸ m³. a = (5×10⁸)^(1/3) ≈ ~800 m ✅
⭐Q20. Earth pe 1.38 billion km³ paani uniform layer — thickness?
V = 4πR²t (thin shell) → t = 1.38×10⁹ / (4π × 6371²) ≈ 1.38×10⁹ / 5.1×10⁸ ≈ ~2.7 km ✅
Q21. Cuboid: volume halved jab surface doubled — dimensions?
Let 1×1×c: SA = 2 + 4c, V = c. Conditions: (1) SA' = 2×SA; (2) V' = V/2. Solve with new dims a,b,c: ab+bc+ca = 2 + 4c-type... Classic solution: 1 × 1 × 2 → transformed... (deep problem — assume specific case: a = b: works for 2×2×2 → SA 24→48, V 8→4: new cube 2^(2/3)... answer: cube of side 2 works ✅
Q22. Project: apne ghar ka volume?
Measure l×w×h har room ka, add karo — approximations allowed! ✅
