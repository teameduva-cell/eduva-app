❓ Parents ke sawal

Kya ye bachche ko track karta hai?

Sirf padhai-related activity (doubts, tests, time) — jo bachcha khud app mein karta hai. Koi location tracking nahi, koi call/message reading nahi. Privacy policy in-app available hai.

Bachcha PIN kaise nahi tod sakta?

PIN sirf aapke paas rehta hai (app mein hash form mein). Reset bhi sirf aap kar sakte ho — Parent Mode → 🔑 PIN बदलो.

Exam ke din raat ko lock hua toh?

Exam date set karne pe Night Lock khud 3 din pehle relax ho jata hai. Aur aap PIN se kabhi bhi 10 second mein unlock kar sakte ho.

Free hai ya subscription?

Saare parental controls FREE hain — EDUVA mein ads bhi nahi hain, data-selling bhi nahi.
