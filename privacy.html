🔒 1. Privacy Policy

1.1 Introduction

EDUVA ("we", "our", "the app") is a 100% free AI-powered study app for students of Class 6–12. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it. EDUVA is designed to comply with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India).

1.2 Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your name, email address and class/board details when you sign up (via email or Google login).
  • Study progress data: Streaks, XP, homework marks, test scores, saved doubts, formula vault items and app preferences. This is stored locally on your device and, if you are logged in, synced to our secure cloud so you never lose your progress.
  • Content you provide: Questions you ask, photos of doubts you upload, and handwritten notes you choose to submit. Uploaded notes are added to our library only with your explicit consent.
  • Parent contact details (optional): If you use the Parent Report feature, the WhatsApp number you enter is stored only on your device and used only when you tap "Send".

1.3 Device Permissions

EDUVA asks for the following permissions, and only for these purposes:

  • 📷 Camera: To photograph a question for instant doubt solving. Photos are used only to generate the answer and are not shared with advertisers.
  • 🎤 Microphone: To let you ask doubts by voice. Audio is processed to text and not stored permanently.
  • 🖼️ Gallery / Storage: To let you attach a photo of a question from your gallery.
  • 🔊 Text-to-Speech: To read answers aloud ("Edu Sir ko sunen").
You can use the app without granting camera/microphone permissions — typing questions always works.

1.4 How We Use Your Information

  • To answer your doubts and check your homework using AI.
  • To maintain your streaks, levels, leaderboards and certificates.
  • To generate your personal revision reminders, mistake book and study reports.
  • To improve the app based on feedback you choose to send.
We do NOT: show advertisements, sell your data, share your data with marketers, or use third-party analytics trackers.

1.5 Children's Privacy (Under 18)

EDUVA is designed for school students, many of whom are under 18. In line with the DPDP Act, 2023:

  • We collect only the minimum data needed to run the app (name, email, study progress).
  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13 without appropriate guidance; such users should use the app under parental supervision.
  • We request that students inform their parents/guardians before creating an account.
  • Parents can review or delete their child's data at any time by contacting us.
  • Our AI responses are filtered for inappropriate content, and abuse filters are active in chat and community features.

1.6 Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted services to operate EDUVA:

  • Google Firebase – secure login (authentication) and cloud database for syncing your progress.
  • Google Gemini – generates answers to your study questions.
  • YouTube (via links only) – curated video lectures open on youtube.com; we do not embed or host third-party videos.

These providers process data under their own privacy and security standards. We do not permit them to use your data for advertising.

1.7 Data Security & Retention

  • Data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Login is protected by your password; password reset is available via email.
  • Data you enter while logged out stays on your own device and can be cleared by logging out or clearing app/browser data.
  • We keep data only as long as your account is active, unless a longer period is required by law.

1.8 Your Rights

  • Access & correction: View or edit your name, class and board in Profile settings.
  • Deletion: Delete your account and all associated data anytime from Profile → Account settings, or by emailing us. Deletion is permanent.
  • Consent withdrawal: You may withdraw consent for note uploads anytime by requesting removal.
  • Grievance redressal: Contact our grievance officer (the founder) at the email below; we respond within reasonable time as per the DPDP Act.

🧒 3. Child Safety Policy

  • Zero tolerance: Adult content, grooming, cyber-bullying or any behaviour targeting children is strictly prohibited and may be reported to appropriate authorities under applicable Indian law (including the IT Act and POCSO where relevant).
