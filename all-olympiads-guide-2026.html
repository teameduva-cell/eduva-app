🧪 NSEJS Pattern — Solved Original Questions

Q1. Ek car 20 m/s se chal rahi hai. Driver ko 0.5 s reaction time ke baad brake lagane hain, jisse deceleration 5 m/s² hai। Rukne ki total doori?
Solution: Reaction distance = 20×0.5 = 10 m. Braking distance = v²/2a = 400/10 = 40 m. Total = 50 m ✅
Q2. Elements A (atomic no. 12) aur B (atomic no. 17) milke compound banate hain। Formula kya hoga?
Solution: A = Mg (2 valence e⁻ → Mg²⁺), B = Cl (7 valence → Cl⁻). Charges balance: MgCl₂ ✅
Q3. Mitosis mein chromosome number kitne divisions mein same rehta hai aur kis stage par chromatids alag hote hain?
Solution: Number same rehta hai (2n → 2n); sister chromatids anaphase mein alag hote hain. Answer: Anaphase ✅

📐 IOQM Pattern — Solved Original Questions

Q4. 7^2026 ko 5 se divide karne par remainder?
Solution: 7 ≡ 2 (mod 5); powers of 2 mod 5 cycle: 2,4,3,1 (period 4). 2026 mod 4 = 2 → 2² = 4. Remainder = 4 ✅
Q5. Triangle ABC mein AB = AC = 13, BC = 10. Area kya hai?
Solution: Isosceles → height from A bisects BC → h = √(13²−5²) = √144 = 12. Area = ½×10×12 = 60 sq units ✅
Q6. 1 se 100 tak kitne integers hain jo na 4 se na 6 se divisible hain?
Solution: |A₄|=25, |A₆|=16, |A₁₂|=8 → divisible = 25+16−8 = 33. Not divisible = 100−33 = 67 ✅

🧮 NMTC + NSEP + Private Olympiads

Q7. (NMTC-Jr) Ek 2-digit number ke digits ka sum 9 hai. Number ko ulta karne par naya number 27 kam milta hai। Number?
Solution: Number = 10a+b, a+b=9, (10a+b)−(10b+a)=27 → 9(a−b)=27 → a−b=3. Solve: a=6, b=3 → 63 ✅
Q8. (NSEP) Ideal gas ka pressure double aur volume half karne par temperature ka kya hoga?
Solution: PV = nRT → P'V' = (2P)(V/2) = PV → T same. Answer: Unchanged ✅
Q9. (SOF IMO) अगला number: 2, 6, 12, 20, 30, ?
Solution: Differences: 4,6,8,10 → next +12 → 30+12 = 42 ✅ (pattern: n²+n)
Q10. (iOEL) Choose the correctly spelt word: (a) Recieve (b) Receive (c) Riceive (d) Receeve
Solution: Rule: 'i before e except after c' → (b) Receive ✅
Q11. (SilverZone iOS) पौधों में जल का परिवहन किस ऊतक द्वारा होता है?
Solution: जल और खनिजों का परिवहन जाइलम (xylem) द्वारा; भोजन का फ्लोएम द्वारा. Answer: Xylem ✅

❓ FAQs

Kya ye questions actual Olympiad ke hain?

Nahi — ye EDUVA dwara banaye gaye original questions hain jo har Olympiad ke official pattern par based hain. Actual papers ke liye official websites (hbcse.tifr.res.in, amti.in, sofworld.org) dekho — copyright-safe rehne ke liye hum papers host nahi karte.

Konsa Olympiad sabse important hai?

Prestige ke hisaab se: NSEJS (Class 8-10) aur IOQM (Class 8-12) — inke through international level tak ja sakte ho. Private Olympiads certificates aur practice ke liye best hain.

Olympiad aur board exam ka syllabus same hai?

Base same (NCERT) hai, par Olympiad mein concepts ka deeper use hota hai. Board ke saath parallel taiyari ho sakti hai — extra depth sirf Olympiad ke liye.
