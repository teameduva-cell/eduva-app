🧬 Original Solved Questions (NEET Pattern)

Q1. (Biology) Mitochondria mein F₀-F₁ particles kahan paaye jaate hain aur inka kaam kya hai?
Solution: Ye inner mitochondrial membrane par hoti hain — ATP synthesis (oxidative phosphorylation) karti hain. F₀ = proton channel (membrane mein embedded), F₁ = ATP synthase enzyme (matrix ki taraf). Answer: Inner membrane; ATP synthesis ✅
Q2. (Biology) DNA replication mein Okazaki fragments kaise bante hain aur kaun jodta hai?
Solution: Lagging strand mein 5'→3' discontinuous synthesis se chhotte fragments (Okazaki) bante hain; unhe DNA ligase jodta hai (phosphodiester bonds). Answer: Ligase ✅
Q3. (Chemistry) 0.1 M HCl solution ka pH aur pOH kya hoga (25°C)?
Solution: HCl strong acid → [H⁺] = 0.1 → pH = 1; pOH = 14 − 1 = 13 ✅
Q4. (Physics) Ek aankh ka least distance of distinct vision 25 cm hai। Simple microscope (convex lens, f = 5 cm) ka maximum magnification final image at 25 par?
Solution: m = 1 + D/f = 1 + 25/5 = 6 ✅
Q5. (Biology) किस hormone को "stress hormone" कहते हैं और यह किस gland से निकलता है?
Solution: Cortisol — adrenal cortex (सम्मतिचर्म) से. Emergency/stress mein release hota hai (glucose metabolism badhata hai). Answer: Cortisol, Adrenal cortex ✅
Q6. (Biology) Incomplete dominance ka example kaunsa hai?
Solution: Mirabilis jalapa (4 o'clock plant) mein red × white → pink flowers. F₁ intermediate phenotype. Answer: Mirabilis (pink flower) ✅
Q7. (Chemistry) Beryllium chloride (BeCl₂) ka hybridization aur shape kya hai gas phase mein?
Solution: Be ke paas 2 bond pairs, no lone pair → sp hybridization, linear shape ✅
